Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 July 2026

Offshore Tactics is a sailing strategy game. This policy explains what data the game handles and why. It is written to be read, not to hide behind legalese.

Playing as a guest

You can play the entire game without an account. In guest mode your campaign is saved only on your own device (using the browser or device's local storage). Nothing is sent to us, there is no login, and no personal data leaves your device.

Creating an account

If you choose to sign in, we use Supabase (our backend provider) to authenticate you with an email address and password. Creating an account lets your campaign sync across your devices and lets you post times to the global leaderboard.

What we store when you have an account

  • Your account — your email address and an encrypted password, held by Supabase's authentication service.
  • Your game save — your campaign progress (funds, boats, crew, race history and settings), stored as a single record in our Supabase Postgres database.
  • Leaderboard entries — for races you finish, your chosen display name and your finishing times/positions, so they can appear on the public leaderboard.

Every record is protected by row-level security, so you can only read and write your own account data. Leaderboard entries are readable by everyone (that is the point of a leaderboard); everything else is private to you.

Your display name

The display name you set is shown publicly on the leaderboard next to your times. Please don't use your real name if you'd rather stay anonymous — any nickname works.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not use third-party advertising or ad-tracking.
  • We do not build advertising profiles or share your data with brokers.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from within the app (Profile → Account → Delete account). Deleting your account removes your cloud save, your profile and your leaderboard entries from our database, and clears the game's cache on that device.

Children

Offshore Tactics is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
